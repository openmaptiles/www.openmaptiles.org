Rooted in open-source history

OpenMapTiles builds on OSM2VectorTiles, a project that won the OpenStreetMap Innovation Award in 2016. It originated in Switzerland as a bachelor’s thesis at OST University, developed in cooperation with Klokan Technologies.

The schema design was influenced by the cartography of the Positron basemap, developed with Paul Norman and the Wikimedia Foundation. Today, the project is maintained and supported by the MapTiler team.
