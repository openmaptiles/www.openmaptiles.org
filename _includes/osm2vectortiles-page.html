OSM2VectorTiles

OSM2VectorTiles was a pioneering open-source project and the direct predecessor of OpenMapTiles. It simplified the creation and hosting of free world maps powered by OpenStreetMap, laying the foundation for modern web-based vector map tile rendering and delivery.

The project was born as the bachelor's thesis of Manuel Roth and Lukas Martinelli from the University of Applied Sciences Rapperswil, Switzerland (now OST), supported and made possible by Klokan Technologies (now MapTiler).

Recognized for its technological innovation and community impact, OSM2VectorTiles won the prestigious OpenStreetMap Innovation Award in 2016.
